# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and patient in all discussions.

## Expected behavior

- Focus on technical issues, not people
- Share reproducible details when reporting problems
- Assume good intent and collaborate in good faith

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks
- Repeated spam or intentionally disruptive behavior

Maintainers may moderate or close threads that violate these expectations.
